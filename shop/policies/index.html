Kissed prints

Shipping, returns & privacy

Kissed prints are made, kissed and packed by me, Mimi (@foxypixiepie). Questions about an order go to hello@foxypixiepie.com.

What you get

  • A photo print in the size you choose, kissed by hand in lipstick, centered on the spot you picked (or wherever I like, if you let me choose).
  • A letter written and signed by me.
  • If you add a message for $10, I write it on your print by hand, up to 80 characters.

Every kiss is real, so color and shape vary a little from print to print.

Shipping

  • US only for now, and shipping is free.
  • Orders ship within 7–14 days, because I kiss, sign and pack each one myself.
  • Prints up to 8×10 ship flat in a rigid mailer. 11×14 and posters ship rolled in a tube.

Returns and damage

  • Each print is personalized and kissed for you, so all sales are final.
  • If your print arrives damaged, email hello@foxypixiepie.com with a photo within 14 days of delivery and I'll send a new one.
  • If it never arrives, email me and I'll check the tracking with you.

Privacy

  • Payments are handled by Stripe. I never see your card number.
  • I use your name, email and shipping address only to make, ship and ask about your order, and the name for your letter only to write it.
  • I don't sell or share your information with anyone, except the carrier that delivers your print.
  • To have your information deleted, email hello@foxypixiepie.com.
